The
Gallery.

A photographic exhibition of culinary work. Each dish has its own dedicated visual field while a deeper 3D environment of plate-like rings, liquid light, floating crumbs, flour dust and glass-like droplets travels continuously through the collection.

Selected Work

DISH PHOTOGRAPHS · 01—08
01
Citrus-Kissed Cream Cheese Mango Symphony

A premium layer cake with tangy cream cheese frosting, delicate mint and lemon zest, paired with tropical mango, juicy fruit and fine pastry elements for a bright, layered dessert experience.

02
Dark Chocolate Salted Caramel Popcorn Cake

A dark chocolate mirror-glazed sponge finished with white chocolate roses and a crown of glossy salted caramel popcorn, balancing deep cocoa, creamy sweetness and a crisp caramelized texture.

03
Rosogolla

Soft, porous chhena dumplings gently poached in fragrant sugar syrup, offering a delicate, cloud-like texture with sweet milky notes, rosewater and subtle cardamom.

04
Shahi Rasamalai Fusion Cake

Soft vanilla sponge soaked in saffron-infused milk and layered with rich Rasamalai cream, finished with rose petals and pistachios for a fragrant Indian-fusion dessert.

05
Baked Cheese and Butterscotch Dried Fruit Tart

A personal-sized tart with flaky buttery pastry, rich baked cheese filling and dried fruits and nuts bound in glossy deep-golden butterscotch, presented with sauce and whole garnish.

06
Cassava Halwa

A rich traditional South Asian cassava sweet made with ghee and jaggery or sugar, with a dense, fudgy and chewy texture, roasted nuts and warm caramelized aromas.

07
Caramel Macaron Trio on Lemon Buttercream Swirl

Three delicate caramel-infused macarons arranged over piped lemon buttercream rosettes with caramel sauce, combining crisp-chewy shells, airy cream and bright citrus sweetness.

08
Dark Christmas Plum Cake

A rich, moist festive cake with dense spiced crumb and candied fruits, delivering warm holiday spice, caramelized richness and a deep fruity sweetness.
